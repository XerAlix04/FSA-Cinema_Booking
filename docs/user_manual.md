# StarView Cinemas - User Manual

## 1. Customer Guide
### 1.1 Booking a Ticket
1. Browse the homepage to see currently active movies. Click a movie poster to view the trailer and details.
2. Select a Cinema Room, Date, and Showtime.
3. On the Seat Map, select your desired seats (Standard, VIP, or Sweetbox). Unavailable seats will be grayed out.
4. Click "Tiếp tục". Your seats are now locked for 5 minutes.

### 1.2 Using Vouchers and Loyalty Points
1. On the Payment screen, if you are logged in, you will see your Loyalty Points balance.
2. Use the `+` and `-` buttons to trade points for Popcorn and Drinks.
3. Enter a valid promotional code (e.g., `WELCOME`) in the Voucher box and click "Áp dụng".
4. Click "Thanh toán VNPay". Upon successful payment, your QR code ticket will appear on screen and be sent to your email.

## 2. Staff Guide
### 2.1 Managing Movies
1. Log in with a `STAFF` account and navigate to the Dashboard.
2. Navigate to "Quản lý Phim". Here you can Add, Edit, or temporarily Disable movies from appearing on the public site.

### 2.2 Scheduling Showtimes
1. Navigate to "Quản lý Suất Chiếu".
2. Select a Movie, a Room, and a Start Time. The system will automatically calculate the end time based on the movie's duration and prevent you from double-booking a room.

## 3. Admin Guide
### 3.1 Managing Staff
1. Log in with an `ADMIN` account.
2. Navigate to "Quản lý Nhân Viên". You can securely generate new accounts for your cinema employees here.

### 3.2 Managing Vouchers & Pricing
1. Navigate to "Quản lý Khuyến Mãi". Create new campaigns (Percentage or Flat discounts). Check the "Thành viên mới" box to restrict the code to first-time buyers.
2. Navigate to "Cấu hình Giá". Select specific showtimes to apply pricing coefficients (e.g., setting a coefficient of `1.15` applies a 15% surcharge for weekend prime-time viewing).
